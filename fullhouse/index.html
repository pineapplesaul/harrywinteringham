    How it works

    No group chat. Just show up.

    01

    Your taste

    Pick films you love, genres, how you like to watch and where you live. It takes two minutes.

    02

    Your house

    We match you into a house of eight at a screening you'll like, with people who like what you like.

    03

    Bring your own ticket

    Book your own seat at the cinema, the way you always do. Full House is your night pass on top:

    • Your ticket, your seat: bought from the cinema as normal
    • Your Full House night pass: your house, the chat and the perks
    • Your first drink at a partner pub near the cinema, before the film
    04

    The seats fill

    Watch the house fill up. Every new seat unlocks the next perk for the whole house.

    05

    Show up

    Grab your free drink at the bar, meet your house, and watch the film together.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Do I have to go with people I don't know?

    That's the idea, but you're never thrown in cold. Your house is matched on taste, the chat opens before the film, and you meet at the bar with a free drink in hand. You can always bring a friend into your house too.

    Is Full House a dating app?

    No. Houses are groups of eight film lovers. If you'd like a one-to-one outing, Cinema Date is optional: you choose whether you're after a date or a film buddy.

    How do you match people?

    On the films and genres you love, the ratings you give after each screening, and your habits: when you like to go, where you sit, and whether you stay for the credits. The more you rate, the better your houses fit.

    Do I still buy a cinema ticket?

    Yes. You book your own seat at the cinema, the way you normally would. Full House is the night pass on top: your house, the chat, the perks and your first drink at a partner pub near the cinema.

    What's the free drink?

    One drink each, picked up before the film. Soft drinks are always on the list.

    Where are you launching?

    We're starting with a pilot in one UK city. Join the waitlist and tell us where you are, and we'll use it to choose where we go next.

    What happens to my details?

    We use them to run your account and match you. Marketing emails are opt-in only, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
