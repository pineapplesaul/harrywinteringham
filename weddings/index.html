Documentary sensibilities, applied to your wedding day.

I spend my working life directing documentaries for the BBC and Channel 4, telling real stories about real people. I bring those same instincts to your wedding. A look across the room. A quiet smile when nobody else is watching. The small, honest moments that make up the real story of your day.

I'm there to fade into the background and capture what actually happens, the way it actually feels. The full story of the day, from the nerves in the morning to the chaos on the dance floor. Your wedding will be one of the best days of your life, so I want to make sure I capture it without getting in the way.
