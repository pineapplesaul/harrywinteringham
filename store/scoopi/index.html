Drop, pick, scoop.

Press the centre and slide left to bring things in: a scan from your iPhone, a grab of anything on your screen, or files. Slide right to choose what comes out: Excel, CSV, a PDF, your own form, or rows pasted straight into an open sheet. Press the centre to scan, check what it found, then press again and the file lands next to the dial.

Scan with your iPhone.

Press Photo on the dial and pick your iPhone. It opens straight into the document scanner, which finds each receipt's edges and flattens it, then sends it to Scoopi. Scan a handful in a row and they land in the tray one by one. There's nothing to install on the phone.

Your own form, filled in.

Drop in your company's expense form once. Scoopi finds where everything goes, replaces placeholders like 0.00 and N, keeps your formulas, and the totals add up.

Questions

What counts as a scan?

One photo, screen grab or PDF page that Scoopi reads. A three-page PDF is three scans. If a read fails, it isn't charged.

Will it work with my company's form?

Most likely. Scoopi fills Excel and CSV files, and PDF forms whether or not they have boxes to type in. It replaces placeholders such as 0.00, N or “(initials)”, and leaves your labels and formulas alone.

What happens to my receipts?

They're sent over an encrypted connection to Claude, the AI from Anthropic, to be read. The filled-in file is made on your Mac. We don't keep copies of your documents.

It got something wrong. What now?

Cross it out in the list beside the dial, or fix it in the file. Check forms before you send them, as you would anyway. Photos taken straight on, in good light, read best.

Is it on Windows or iPhone?

Mac only for now. Windows is on its way: leave your email and we'll tell you when. Your iPhone does the scanning, though: press Photo on the dial, pick your iPhone, and it opens its document scanner. The scans land straight in Scoopi.
