Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Scoopi is run by Earwig AI Ltd (“we”), the company behind earwig.io, and registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

What we keep

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send your sign-in links.
  • Your scans balance and purchases: how many scans you've bought and used, and when.
  • Usage counts for each read: how many pages, and how much it cost us, so we can keep prices fair.
  • Your email, if you join the Windows waitlist, only to tell you when Scoopi for Windows is ready. We delete the list once that email has gone out.

Your documents

When you make a file, the photos, screen grabs and documents you added are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and on to Claude, the AI service from Anthropic, to be read. We don't store your documents or what Claude reads from them. The file Scoopi makes is created on your Mac and stays there until you save or share it. Anthropic processes the documents under its commercial terms.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Who else is involved

Supabase (sign-in and our database), Anthropic (reading documents), Stripe (payments) and Cloudflare (this website).

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete your account, at hi@harrywinteringham.uk.
