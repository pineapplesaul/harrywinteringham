Educating Yorkshire wins an International Emmy

Educating Yorkshire has won an International Emmy Award.

I was a Producer Director on the series for Two Four and Channel 4, back at Thornhill Academy. Much of my time was spent with the teachers, and with students who have special educational needs, building the trust that let them tell their own stories.

Thank you to the staff, the students and their families for letting us in, and to everyone at Two Four and Channel 4 who made the series.
